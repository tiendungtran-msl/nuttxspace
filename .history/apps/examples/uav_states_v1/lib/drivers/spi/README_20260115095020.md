## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Pure NuttX** - No external dependencies (PX4-free)
- ✅ **Thread-Safe** - Multiple locking modes (NONE, THREADS, PREEMPTION)
- ✅ **High Performance** - Direct macro access, minimal overhead (~4μs per transfer)
- ✅ **Type-Safe** - C++17 with strong typing
- ✅ **Easy to Use** - Helper functions for register access
- ✅ **Flexible** - Dynamic frequency switching
- ✅ **Robust** - Write verification, read-modify-write support
- ✅ **Well Documented** - Doxygen-style comments
